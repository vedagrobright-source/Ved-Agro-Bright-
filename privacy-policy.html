Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 • Official document for Ved Agro Bright

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ved Agro Bright. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our educational portal to access notes, quiz modules, and updates.

2. Information We Process

We believe in keeping things transparent. Our portal does not collect highly confidential personal information. However, when you browse our platform, participate in our practice quizzes, or access study materials, minimal technical log details may be processed:

  • Log Files (Standard browser type, ISP, date/time stamp).
  • Device Information (Screen size and operating system for responsive layout optimization).
  • Cookies and Web Beacons to save basic interface preferences.

3. Cookies Policy

Ved Agro Bright uses basic cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses, and to customize our web page layout to deliver a smooth and fast mobile user experience.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement industry-standard practices and rely on secure cloud frameworks to protect the portal from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure of technical study logs.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for students and children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and monitor their children's online study activity. Ved Agro Bright does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent

By using our website and accessing our educational materials, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its standard terms.
